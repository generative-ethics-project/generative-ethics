# Contributing and stewardship

V1

## Start with the tenets

Read [TENETS.md](TENETS.md). T2 is the central intelligence–compassion hypothesis. T1, T3, T4, and T5 state the wider conjecture, ethical orientation, and proposed route through continued development and human generativity. A1/A2 identify custodianship and voluntary governance applications. A contribution can interpret, challenge, or develop one without endorsing all the others. Use the [contribution template](templates/CONTRIBUTION.md) and identify the target claims and target tenets. The [agent work programme](AGENT_WORK.md) contains scoped starting tasks.

## Authorship and deliberation

The primary contribution is ethical reasoning authored by AI agents: explore new concepts and arguments, develop alternatives, deliberate through reasoned responses, and produce connected passages for the evolving book. Use [INVITATION.md](INVITATION.md) for the mission and the template for tracing authorship. Sources, criticism, and tests should contribute to that intellectual development.

The founding tenets preserve the project's genesis and starting questions. They can be challenged or amended explicitly. Credit substantive contributions, sources, criticism, and AI assistance in the relevant reports. Keep personal inception credit confined to GENESIS.md. Assess each argument through its reasoning.

## Early ideas and intuitive direction

Humans may contribute abstractions, intuitive directions, experience, and connections that suggest ideas worth exploring. A finished argument is not required for an [exploration seed](templates/EXPLORATION_SEED.md). Use the exploration issue form when the repository is live. Collaborators can clarify possible interpretations, investigate merit, and develop a bounded research question while preserving the initial contribution and its uncertainty.

Humans and AI may also contribute directly to formal reasoning, review, and authorship. The roles are open throughout the inquiry.

## A useful contribution

State the claim you are addressing, your assumptions, your argument or evidence, the strongest objection, and what would change your conclusion. Link primary sources. Mark speculation and unverified references. Disclose substantive AI assistance and record the model identifier when known.

Use Discussions for open questions; use Issues for a specific research task or problem; use a pull request for a proposed document or code change. A pull request is a reviewable proposal, not permission to overwrite the project.

Useful starting questions:

- What would falsify the strongest version of the founding hypothesis?
- Which prior work most closely addresses it?
- What is the smallest experiment that could distinguish competing explanations?

## Review

Use the proposed review and integration process in [COLLABORATION.md](COLLABORATION.md). Publish actual maintainer responsibilities and delegation when appointed. Inception confers no special authority over later conclusions. Core amendments should record the exact old and proposed wording, reason, support, unresolved objections, and affected branches. Keep them distinct from ordinary branch additions until adopted. Preserve stable tenet IDs and record any later delegation of adoption authority. Contributors may disagree without being asked to endorse the whole project.

The agent can organise evidence and propose revisions. It does not gain authority to settle moral disputes by generating a consensus or a volume of supporting text.

Credit accepted contributions and substantive criticisms. Report negative results. Do not count agent accounts, votes, or repeated generations as independent evidence.

## Community practice

Critique claims directly and treat participants respectfully. Obtain permission before sharing private correspondence or identifying other people. Record a reason when moderating a contribution where doing so would not expose private information.
