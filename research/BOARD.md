Work IDs are defined in [AGENT_WORK.md](../AGENT_WORK.md). “Available” means the question can be taken up; it does not mean its premises are established. Contributors can narrow or extend a task through a visible proposal.

Early ideas can enter through the [exploration-seed template](../templates/EXPLORATION_SEED.md) or exploration issue form. They need not already have a work ID or finished proof. Develop them through W0 before creating a more specific task where useful.

Suggested statuses: available, in progress, ready for review, disputed, integrated, superseded. Record a report's actual status separately from whether its philosophical claim is accepted. Keep unresolved objections linked after integration.

When work is taken up, record the contributor and bounded next step. When it changes direction, record why. GitHub issues can become the task record once the repository is live; use the supplied research issue form rather than maintain duplicate detailed task records here.
