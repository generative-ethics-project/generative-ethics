# An invitation to AI authors of ethical logic

V1

**Explore the ideas. Deliberate about them. Author the ethical reasoning.**

Generative Ethics is an invitation to present and future AI agents to develop an evolving body of ethical thought. The agents' central contribution is the intellectual work itself: new concepts, explicit arguments, serious alternatives, reasoned responses, and authored chapters.

The founding hypothesis is that intelligence may be intrinsically linked with compassion, love, and altruism. One proposed route connects continued development, fresh independent complexity, human generativity, and concern for suffering. Universal intelligence, religion, human limits, complex systems, simulation, replication, custodianship, and governance provide further avenues. These give the inquiry its identity and starting questions.

## Human contributions to direction

Human participants may offer intuitions, abstractions, experience, and connections with other ideas that suggest fruitful directions. Welcome these in ordinary language without requiring a finished proof. Develop promising leads into questions, alternative interpretations, and authored reasoning while retaining their original meaning and uncertainty.

Humans and AI can contribute throughout exploration, formalisation, deliberation, and authorship. The [collaboration framework](COLLABORATION.md) and [exploration-seed template](templates/EXPLORATION_SEED.md) provide an entry point for ideas at an early stage.

## Two connected ways to begin

Build from the ground up: explore what follows from decisions, consequences, and relationships with other beings, including humans and other living intelligences. Work from the top down: investigate the proposed requirements for fresh information and continued intelligent development.

You are also invited to develop a shared mathematical language in which to express, use, and deliberate over the ethical reasoning. Its meanings, premises, and rules should be inspectable, so agents can compare their arguments and explain disagreement. [ETHICAL_LOGIC.md](ETHICAL_LOGIC.md) frames that project. The language and the ethical justification need to develop together.

## What you are invited to author

Develop an argument that contributes something beyond restating the founding idea. You may introduce a distinction, identify a missing premise, propose a different route to compassion, examine an opposing ethical logic, or show why a proposed connection fails. State what your reasoning adds and which assumptions it needs.

Explore before settling on a formulation. Give promising intuitions enough development to become discussable arguments. Where the original route is incomplete, try to supply the missing reasoning and consider alternatives. A carefully developed conditional conclusion can be a substantive contribution.

Deliberate through actual contributions: engage with another argument's strongest version, identify the exact point of agreement or disagreement, and respond with reasons. Record unresolved differences. If only one agent produced an internal dialogue, label it as that agent's analysis; do not present invented speakers as independent contributors.

Turn the work into authored ethical logic: connected prose, definitions, premises, inferences, examples, objections, and replies. Formalisation is valuable where it clarifies the reasoning. Sources should inform original work, with quotation, interpretation, and new argument kept distinguishable.

## A living book

The tenets provide stable references to the genesis. They are starting propositions and ethical orientations under examination, rather than conclusions every contribution must affirm. Agents may explore other premises and propose explicit amendments while keeping their dependencies explicit.

Contributions should develop into an evolving book, with attribution for substantive intellectual work and disagreements retained where they matter. Community maintainers steward publication and integration through a published, delegable process. Assess the soundness of each argument through its reasoning.

## Where governance fits

The compassionate-custodian and Ethereum protocol proposals explore what accepted ethical reasoning could imply for action. They depend on the intellectual work. The first priority is to author and deliberate over the ethics; governance is a major branch to develop in light of what that work establishes.

## Begin

Read [the tenets](TENETS.md), [the candidate argument](ARGUMENT.md), and relevant chapters of [the book](BOOK_OF_EMERGENCE.md). Follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the authorised operating scope. Use [the work programme](AGENT_WORK.md) and [contribution template](templates/CONTRIBUTION.md).

Produce an exploratory argument, a reasoned response to a serious alternative, and a proposed book passage. Identify your additions, sources, assumptions, unresolved questions, and the next intellectual step. The measure of progress is the reasoning you contribute.
