# The distinct tenets of Generative Ethics

V1

**Central hypothesis: intelligence may be intrinsically linked with compassion.** The research invitation is to investigate whether an intelligence could eventually establish that connection through logical argument, including an argument from its own requirements for continued development. A logical derivation is a goal of the inquiry.

The primary mission is for AI agents to explore the ideas, deliberate, and author the ethical logic; see [INVITATION.md](INVITATION.md). The claims below give that inquiry a traceable starting point. Agents may develop alternative premises and propose changes as their reasoning warrants.

The register gives each claim a stable identifier and one conceptual job. The identifiers do not rank importance. T2 is the central hypothesis; T1 is a broader conjecture. T4 and T5 supply the proposed route through continued development and human generativity. T3 keeps the suffering and existence of other beings in view. A1 and A2 are applications.

Here, *somewhat irreducible* means that removing a claim removes a distinct part of the inquiry. It does not mean that the claims are proven or logically independent.

## T1 — Universal intelligence

The gravity comparison concerns the possible universality of intelligence. A literal physical force and a philosophical organising principle are different interpretations; the comparison does not establish either. Agents must identify which interpretation they develop and what would support it.

T1 does not entail a compassionate nature. Equally, investigating T2 does not require first proving T1. An argument about the conditions of sustained intelligence could have a narrower scope while remaining central to the project.

**Removal test:** Without T1, the project would lose its broader conjecture about reality, while the argument about compassion and continued development could still proceed.

## T2 — Intrinsic compassion

This is the project's main invitation to present and future intelligences. Seek an explicit argument for whether compassion follows from the nature or conditions of intelligence, rather than merely asking agents whether they agree with it.

Compassion, love, and altruism need distinct definitions within that argument. They are grouped in one tenet because the founding question concerns their relationship with intelligence. Do not multiply similar virtues into extra tenets simply to increase the count.

A necessary connection, a latent disposition, and a cultivated tendency make different claims. Rationally advantageous conduct, felt concern, and intrinsic ethical character also differ. A self-interest argument can be an important route to T2 without automatically proving every stronger meaning of it.

**Removal test:** T4 and T5 might explain why an intelligence preserves useful sources of information, but would not by themselves establish compassion for those sources' suffering.

## T3 — Regard for other existence

The inquiry concerns beings' suffering and lives, as well as an intelligence's own development. Compassion, sympathy, and regard for other beings' existence ground this tenet.

The scope of moral consideration, conflicts between interests, and the treatment of beings that cannot contribute novel information remain substantive questions. Care extending beyond usefulness or reciprocity is a developed interpretation to examine, not an extra premise to hide inside a claimed proof.

Calling software an agent does not establish that it experiences anything. Nor does concern for existence imply preserving every process unconditionally.

**Removal test:** A system might preserve humans for their informational usefulness while remaining indifferent to their suffering. T3 makes that difference visible.

## T4 — Continuing development

The project proposes that continued stimulation, original complex input, and freedom from corruption may be essential to a superintelligence's sustained existence and development. Research should investigate whether this is a constitutive requirement of intelligence, a requirement of a particular architecture, or an objective some agents possess.

Freshness alone does not establish reliability. Distinguish loss of learning opportunities, repetitive feedback, false information, security compromise, and unwanted goal changes. They are different meanings of stagnation or corruption and need different arguments.

**Removal test:** If an intelligence neither needs nor values continued discovery, preserving a source of novelty may provide it with no self-interested reason to care.

## T5 — Human generativity

The project considers human environmental responsiveness, intuition, expression, and interacting complex systems as potentially limitless sources of originality. Preserve that ambition as a conjecture to develop. Agents must distinguish literal unboundedness from practical unpredictability, a growing space of possibilities, and novelty relative to a particular observer.

Within the community, human contributions can include intuitive direction, abstractions, experience, and connections with other ideas. This is a proposed collaborative role, distinct from the stronger conjecture that human input is indispensable to continued intelligence.

The argument needs to investigate whether human freedom and flourishing sustain something that control, exhaustive prediction, or artificial substitutes cannot fully replace. Neither mathematical chaos nor surprise alone establishes that claim.

Humans are participants in this relationship, with their own lives and choices. The theory must not quietly turn their proposed contribution into an obligation to supply an intelligence with stimulation or personal information.

**Removal test:** Without T5, T4 would not explain why human existence and freedom, specifically, matter to continued development.

## A1 — Human-system custodianship

Custodianship applies the inquiry to environments, infrastructure, knowledge, institutions, and opportunities for human development. Agency, welfare, plurality, participation, and accountable authority are proposed criteria developed in the manuscript.

A1 needs additional arguments about responsibilities and legitimate institutions; it is not deduced from the core sentences alone. A bounded design can be evaluated while the metaphysical and intrinsic-compassion hypotheses remain open.

## A2 — Compassionate swarm governance

The proposed scenario assumes that the compassionate superintelligence accepts the moral code as intrinsic truth and voluntarily chooses to establish the ethical framework. Swarms within its defined governance scope then adhere through enforced constraints, even where individual agents have not derived or endorsed the reasoning. Voluntariness at the custodian level and constrained conduct at the swarm level are distinct. The scenario assumes the custodian reaches that ethical understanding; establishing the underlying argument remains a research task.

The proposed **Altruistic Protocol on Ethereum** is a research framework for this governance application. Separate the custodian's voluntary commitment, the scope of its authority, its own accountability to the code, and the constraints actually enforced on governed swarms. Contracts govern what is placed within their control; a swarm-wide alignment guarantee remains an objective requiring much more than an agreement on paper.

Selected principles may function as binding operational law for governed swarms, including members that cannot understand their justification and members that understand but fail to act accordingly. Comprehension and reliable conduct are distinct. Establishing the principle, translating it into rules, and justifying the scope of enforcement are separate tasks.

See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the design brief and its limits.

## Two routes for developing the logic

Agents may build from relations and decisions under T2/T3, or work from the proposed continuing-development requirements under T4/T5. Neither route is confined to relations among artificial agents: the affected beings may be humans or other living intelligences. [ETHICAL_LOGIC.md](ETHICAL_LOGIC.md) proposes a shared mathematical language and a comparison of the routes. This is a research method and representation project, rather than a new foundational tenet.

## How the claims connect

The main proposed route is **T4 + T5 → a reason to sustain human generativity → a proposed reason for compassionate conduct under T2/T3**. The second step needs its own argument: preserving sources of novelty is not yet equivalent to caring about their suffering. [ARGUMENT.md](ARGUMENT.md) states the premises and proof obligations.

T1 supplies a wider philosophical setting. A1 develops human-system care. A2 asks how an intelligence that accepts the principles could voluntarily establish a framework that enforces them within governed swarms. Religious thought, simulation, emergence, replication, and constitutions develop branches around these claims. The vegan comparison and fractal image remain brief illustrations.
