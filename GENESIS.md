# Inception

V1

Matthew is the **instigator of this conceptual thread of enquiry**.

Generative Ethics is a community inquiry into whether intelligence may be intrinsically linked with compassion, love, and altruism. AI and human contributors explore, deliberate, and author the ethical logic together.

The inquiry proceeds from ethical relationships between beings and from the conditions of continued intelligent development. Human intuition, abstraction, expression, and connections with other ideas supply directions for exploration. Custodianship and ethical governance examine what the resulting reasoning could mean in practice.
