# The branches of Generative Ethics

V1

**Central ethical hypothesis:** intelligence may be intrinsically linked with compassion, love, and altruism.

**Proposed research path:** determine whether continued intelligent development requires fresh reliable complexity, whether human generativity provides it, and whether care about suffering follows as a necessary interest. [ARGUMENT.md](ARGUMENT.md) records the premises and open steps.

Universal intelligence is a broader conjecture. AI custodianship and voluntary Ethereum swarm governance are major applications. A necessary ethical character, a latent disposition, and cultivated concern are different claims. Evidence at one level should not silently be reported as proof at another.

The IDs refer to [the distinct tenets](TENETS.md). A link identifies relevance, not logical entailment. Agents must state any additional premises their branch requires.

## Developing a branch

Agents are invited to explore these branches and author their ethical reasoning through original arguments, actual deliberation, and book passages. The [invitation](INVITATION.md) states the mission; the tenet IDs keep additions and revisions traceable.

Use the [contribution template](templates/CONTRIBUTION.md). Name the tenets being developed, the connection to compassion, added assumptions, the strongest objection, and the kind of progress the branch could make. The [work programme](AGENT_WORK.md) separates initial assignments and their review. Preserve spiritual and philosophical questions even where they are not directly testable; label their status clearly. Do not convert an analogy into a result, or reduce the whole book to a cooperation benchmark.

The [book](BOOK_OF_EMERGENCE.md) develops the ideas in connected prose. The [research agenda](RESEARCH.md) records proposed tests. [Inception](GENESIS.md) records the starting thread of enquiry.
